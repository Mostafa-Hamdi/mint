MINT CONSENT

MINT CONSENT is a digital consent platform. It transforms the informed consent process by providing patients with clear, accessible information before they agree to a medical procedure or treatment.

MINT CONSENT

Instead of relying solely on a single consultation, patients can review procedure information, benefits, risks, alternatives, and aftercare instructions at their own pace, from home or on their mobile device. This empowers patients and allows them to arrive at decisions confidently and at their own pace.

MINT CONSENT

For healthcare professionals, MINT CONSENT supports more consistent and structured communication with patients. Standardised procedure information helps ensure that key topics are discussed while allowing clinicians to personalise conversations according to each patient's individual circumstances. Digital consent platforms can also document when information has been provided, when consent forms have been completed, and any questions or concerns raised by the patient, creating a clear record that supports good clinical practice and shared decision-making.

MINT CONSENT

For hospitals and healthcare organisations, MINT CONSENT offers an opportunity to improve efficiency, standardisation and governance across multiple departments and sites. MINT CONSENT and MINT PILs (patient information leaflets) can be centrally managed, updated and distributed, helping to ensure patients receive consistent, high-quality information
