Medical Information Notable and Trusted

MINT PILS

Our interactive MINT PILs were created with the idea of empowering patients with the knowledge they need to take control of their treatment. They discuss general issues in health as well as specific health problems in a simple, yet comprehensive manner, without losing touch with the culture and society the patient is in.

MINT PILs

is one of the services provided by AMPM Training Ltd.

is one of the services provided by AMPM Training Ltd.

AMPM Training was established with a vision of improving healthcare provision through enhancing communication. It operates under a team with over 25 years of experience in healthcare delivery and training medical staff. We aspire to a healthcare culture based on transparency and communication that develops mutual trust and understanding.

MINT PILs

Our MINT PILs division is involved in patient edification, which we believe goes beyond patient education into a more spiritual level of enlightenment. This is important for a holistic form of healing. We have designed our MINT PILs to be visual to capture the social issues and feelings, as well as added interactivity to enhance the user experience. The acronym stands for Medical Information Notable and Trusted. Accordingly, our information is based on the requirements of the community and is based on the latest guidance and is all referenced. We are working with PIF Tick, “The UK’s quality mark for trusted health information”, to ensure our leaflets are produced using a professional and structured process.
