FAQ

Medical Institutes:

Who writes the information provided in the MINT PILS?

Is the information up-to-date and accurate?

Is the information applicable to the community, I live in?

All MINT PILs are reviewed and altered in both content and illustrations, to suit the community it is distributed in.

How can I offer this service to my patients?

Please contact us on info@ampmtraining.com to arrange an online meeting with you.

What if I have concerns regarding the information in the MINT PILS?

Please contact us on support, and we will look into your concerns, and get back to you.

Can my patients give feedback about the MINT PILS?

All MINT PILs have a feedback page at the end of the leaflet, to allow patients to share their thoughts. They also have the option to give feedback on the digital platform itself, and of course we always welcome emails on info@ampmtraining.com

How is the MINT PIL interactive?

Each MINT PIL has extra features to clarify medical issues, such as animations, extra information to explain related content, or voice overs to read the MINT PILs for you.

Patients:

How can I access the MINT PILS?

Please visit your health care provider and ask for a link to the MINT PIL you need.

Do I need to register to view the MINT PILS?

No, you do not need to register. You can log in a as a guest and view your MINT PIL without registering.

How is my personal information going to be used?

- We only need your name, email address or phone number to use as a username. All MINT PILS assigned to you will be linked to the username. - We do not sell personal information to any third-party companies. - Anonymous information regarding the use of the MINT PILs may be used for research and development.

What are the benefits of registering?

- You can see all your MINT PILs at the same platform. As a guest you can only see one PIL at a time. - You do not need to look for the link every time you need to access it.

Why can't I access my MINT PIL again?

- If you have opened your MINT PIL on another device, you will not be able to open the link. All MINT PILs can only be viewed on one device (the one you open it on first). - All MINT PILs links have an expiry date which may range from a few days to a few month. It is possible your link has expired. If you need to access the information, contact your healthcare provider to send you a new link.

How do I register?

You need to provide the same contact that you have received your link on (email address or phone number) and follow the steps on the link.

Why can't I log in?

- Check that you have the same email address/phone number that you have registered with. - Check that you have the correct caps lock for the password. - If you are still unable to log in, please contact us on info@ampmtraining.com

Why can't I find my MINT PIL when I log in?

The MINT PIL is linked to the contact info that was provided to your health care provider. This same contact info is your username if you choose to register. You will need to login with the same email address or phone number that the link has been sent to, in order to see the MINT PIL.

Why has my MINT PIL disappeared?

Our MINT PILs are constantly getting reviewed to match the latest medical research, and we are constantly updating the leaflets to increase their interactive features. As such, our MINT PILs are time limited to ensure that you have access to the most recent information. If you require access to the MINT PIL again, please ask your doctor for a new link.

Where can I find trusted further readings?

Each MINT PIL comes with its references and further readings carefully selected to compliment the information leaflets. These readings all come from notable medical entities from the UK, USA, Australia, and Europe.

How can I provide feedback for the platform?

At the top right corner, go to the Feedback and click on it. You should get a form to fill out your feedback regarding the platform.

How can I provide feedback for a specific MINT PIL?

At the end of the MINT PIL, you will get a chance to provide feedback on the MINT PIL just viewed.

Can I share a MINT PIL?

No, each MINT PIL can only be accessed by one device.

How is the MINT PIL interactive?

Each MINT PIL has extra features to explain more if needed, such as animations, extra information to explain related content, or voice overs to read the MINT PILs for you.
