MINT CONSENT

This is a digital consent platform. It focuses on giving the patient the relevant information needed to ensure an informed decision. Every aspect of consent is explained in easy to understand language. The consent is fully adjustable to suit the needs of your patient. You can choose to attach our PIF Tick certified MINT PILs.

MINT PILs

Our MINT PILs (Medical Information Notable and Trusted) are easy-to-read, illustrated leaflets designed for mobile use. They have videos and interactive elements incorporated within the PILs. They also have read-only and read-aloud versions to suit every need. For further information please go to AMPMTraining.com.

MINT FAMILY

This is our app designed for patients and their families. It allows them to keep all the information they need in one place and to communicate with each other in health matters.
