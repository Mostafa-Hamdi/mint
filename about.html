About AMPM Training

About AMPM Training

AMPM Training is a UK-
based digital health education company headquartered in Wellington, Somerset.
It was established in 2020.
We specialise in digital patient education solutions that enhance health literacy and empower healthcare professionals to
deliver better care.

AMPM Training provides hospitals, pharmaceutical companies, and EMR vendors with trusted, compliant digital patient
education solutions.
We help hospitals to improve patient satisfaction, safety, and health literacy metrics while aligning with CQC, CBAHI, JCI, and
GAHAR standards.

Our solutions also support pharmaceutical brands in delivering patient-centered, regulatory-compliant materials, and
provide EMR vendors with value-added modules that enhance their platforms and differentiate their offerings.

Message from our CEO

When patient education moved online, something important got left behind: accessibility and connection.
I founded AMPM Training because I saw a widening gap between what patients need and what they are
actually receiving.
In 2013, the UK’s push for a paperless NHS removed printed PILs from consultations. PDFs took their place.
however, they are harder to find, harder to absorb, and disconnected from the patients' care journey.
Our goal was clear: to reinvent patient education for the digital era.
A solution that speaks the patient’s language visually, simply, and instantly.
That’s how MINT PILs and the MINT Platform came to life: engaging, interactive content delivered at the
point of care.
Behind every informed patient is someone who cares enough to teach them well. That is the change we
champion every day.

Ahmed Samy Boraei
Chief Executive Officer
AMPM Training
