You can now register individual clinic accounts as opposed to a full institute account. These accounts allow you to send educational content to your patients, unrelated to institutional accounts.

The MINT Platform allows you to access MINT Patient Information Leaflets in your clinic and send them to your patients. The following video shows to use your account to educate patients, set your favourites and send MINT PILs to your patients.

When you use the MINT Platform to send patient information material, you can send a small reminder to your patients with your name, qualifications and contact details. Patients will know and remember the doctor that cares. The following video will show you how to create a Brand Engagement Message with the MINT Team.
